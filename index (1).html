Founder & CEO

Tanmay Rajput

Author • World-Builder • Composer of the Studio's Score

Immortal Paradox Studios is the independent home of Tanmay Rajput's science-fiction universe — a single epic story that begins with a red sky over Washington D.C. and refuses to stop expanding. Every novel, every light novel volume, every track on the music channel, and every frame of the future films begins with the same question: what happens when the world you love turns against you?

The studio exists to give that story a solid foundation — so the universe can grow for years, in every medium, without losing its soul.

Story FirstEvery song, cover, and scene exists to serve the narrative.
World-BuildingMysteries are planted with intent and paid off with patience.
CommunityReaders and listeners shape what the studio builds next.

The Flagship Novel

THE INVADERS (WORKING TITLE)

When the sky turns red over Washington D.C., nineteen-year-old Tony reaches for his camera instead of a weapon. What follows is an escape across a burning city, a kaiju that stops at the border and chooses not to follow, and a consciousness that wakes up inside someone else's body — where the first person he truly sees has silver eyes, and the last thing he remembers is his own name.
